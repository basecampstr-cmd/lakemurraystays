About Lake Murray

South Carolina's crown jewel — "The Jewel of the Midlands."

Completed in 1930, Lake Murray was formed by the Saluda Dam — an earthen structure roughly 1.5 miles long and 220 feet high that, at the time, created the largest man-made reservoir in the world and powered the region with hydroelectricity.

Today it spans four counties just west of Columbia, stretching 41 miles long and up to 14 miles wide, with water reaching nearly 190 feet deep. Its 650 miles of shoreline are dotted with quiet coves, sandy islands, waterfront dining, and some of the best sunset views in the state.

The lake carries real history: during World War II, B-25 Mitchell bombers — including crews connected to the famous Doolittle Raiders — trained over these waters, and several aircraft still rest on the lakebed. One recovered B-25 is now preserved in a museum.

World-Class Fishing

The fishing lake that put the midlands on the map

Lake Murray is a nationally recognized bass fishery and a striped-bass powerhouse. It's the kind of water that draws anglers from across the country — and the kind of travel we already know how to book.

🐟 Striped Bass

Prime · Fall–Spring

Lake Murray's signature fish. Landlocked stripers school and chase bait across open water — a bucket-list target that fuels a full guide economy.

🎣 Largemouth Bass

Prime · Mar–May

A perennial tournament lake. Spring spawn and fall feed windows bring anglers to the coves, points, and brush all season long.

🐠 Blue & Channel Catfish

Prime · Summer Nights

Trophy blue catfish push serious weight here, making Murray a favorite for catfish tournaments and family night trips off the dock.

🐡 Crappie & Bream

Prime · Spring

Slab crappie around brush and bridges plus easy bream action make this the perfect lake for families and first-time anglers.

750,000+

Bomb Island hosts one of the largest purple martin roosts in North America — hundreds of thousands of birds funnel onto the island at dusk each summer. It's a protected sanctuary and one of the most jaw-dropping wildlife spectacles in the Southeast, drawing evening boat tours all season.
