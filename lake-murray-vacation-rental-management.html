Questions

Lake Murray owner FAQ

Are you actually managing homes on Lake Murray yet?

We're launching on Lake Murray now and actively onboarding our first homes. You'd be getting in early — with a team that already has a proven track record and an existing guest network from Lake Hartwell.

How is your pricing structured?

A simple percentage of revenue, no hidden cleaning markups, and no long-term contracts. We earn your business every month. Request a custom quote and we'll walk you through the numbers for your specific home.

What areas of Lake Murray do you serve?

The full lake and its four-county shoreline — from Lexington and Chapin to Irmo, Prosperity, and the Dreher Island area — plus the surrounding Columbia metro.

What makes you different from Vacasa or Evolve?

We're a lake specialist, not a national big-box manager. We market to fishing and lake travelers, run our own branded booking website, bring an existing guest database, and keep a local care team on the water.

Do I have to renovate or add amenities?

Not required — but we'll tell you which high-ROI touches (hot tubs, dock upgrades, game rooms, styling) tend to pay for themselves quickly, exactly as they did in our Lake Hartwell case studies.

How do you handle dock and water levels?

Seasonal water-level management and dock know-how are part of our specialty. We manage guest expectations and safety around changing conditions so reviews stay strong.

What could my home earn?

It depends on location, size, bedrooms, and amenities. The fastest way to find out is a free revenue analysis — fill out the form below and we'll send a custom projection.

How fast can you get my home live?

Once we've walked through your property and finalized photos and pricing, we move quickly to get you listed across all channels and our website.
