Questions

Lake Murray owner FAQ

What exactly is the home-watch program?

Regular, documented checks of your home — interior and exterior, storm and freeze checks, running water, HVAC, dock and shoreline, security — with photo reports sent to you after each visit. It runs whether the home is booked or sitting empty, so your place is protected year-round, not just when there's a guest in it.

Can I get home watch without full rental management?

Yes. If you're not ready to rent — or want your home looked after during the off-season or between owner stays — we offer home-watch-only care. Many owners start there and add rental management once they see how we operate.

Are you actually managing homes on Lake Murray yet?

We're launching on Lake Murray now and actively onboarding our first homes. You'd be getting in early — with a team that already has a proven Lake Hartwell track record and an existing guest network.

How is your pricing structured?

A simple percentage of revenue, no hidden cleaning markups, and no long-term contracts. We earn your business every month. Request a custom quote and we'll walk you through the numbers for your specific home.

What makes you different from Vacasa or Evolve?

We're a lake specialist, not a national big-box manager. We market to fishing and lake travelers, run our own branded booking website that ranks for Lake Murray searches, bring an existing guest database, and keep a local care team on the water.
